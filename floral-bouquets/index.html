A Bouquet Made of Cake

Every rose, carnation and hydrangea in our bouquets is piped by hand in buttercream, then set on a scratch-made cupcake and arranged like a florist’s bouquet. They look like fresh flowers, and they taste even better.

Bouquets make a memorable gift for birthdays, Mother’s Day, showers, anniversaries and thank-yous, and a beautiful centerpiece for any table. Tell us the colors you love and we will design around them.

Questions

How far ahead should I order?

A week’s notice is best, and more for holidays like Mother’s Day and Valentine’s Day. Reach out and we will do our best to fit you in.

How do I keep a bouquet fresh?

Keep it cool and out of direct sun, and enjoy it within a day or two. Buttercream softens in the heat.

Can you match a color scheme?

Yes. Send us your colors or a photo and we will design the flowers to match.
