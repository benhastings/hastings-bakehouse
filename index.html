Welcome

Hastings Bake House is passionate about creating delectable and thoughtfully designed cakes that become a gift and an experience for you and your guests. We use only the highest quality ingredients and say “no” to seed oils and artificial dyes. You can truly taste the difference! From large traditional wedding cakes, to cutting cakes with dessert buffets, we can create any vision you may have.

Your cake is one of the few elements of your celebration that involves all five senses. Hastings Bake House focuses on each one as we consider each custom cake we make. Led by a pastry chef with over 25 years of experience, you can rest easy, knowing we will help guide your selections and create a delicious and memorable experience for you and your guests.

Every cake is a custom piece of art, built on a canvas of delicious, scratch, all-natural ingredients.
